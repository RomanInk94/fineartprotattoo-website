How it works

  1. EnquiryMessage on WhatsApp with the date, the type of event, the location and the approximate number of guests.
  2. Flash sheetRoman designs a flash sheet with 8 to 15 fine line designs for your theme.
  3. Event dayThe station is set up on site. Guests choose a design and are tattooed professionally.
  4. AftercareEvery guest receives aftercare instructions, and the tattoos heal within two to four weeks.

Practical details

Event tattoos are small, typically 3 to 8 cm, and take 15 to 30 minutes each. Usually 30 to 60 percent of guests take part. Roman needs about 2 × 2 m of space, a power socket and good light, and sets up everything else himself.

Sterile single-use needles and materials are used, just as in the studio. Guests who have been drinking are not tattooed. Book four to eight weeks ahead; summer dates go early. Packages and prices on request, depending on hours, number of guests and location in Cyprus.

Event tattoo questions

Are these real tattoos?

Yes, only real, permanent tattoos: no henna, airbrush or stickers. Tattooed professionally with sterile single-use needles.

Is a tattoo station at an event hygienic?

Yes. Sterile single-use needles, medical gloves, disinfected surfaces and single-use materials, just like in the studio.

How much does an event tattoo artist cost in Cyprus?

Packages are quoted individually based on hours, number of guests and location. Send the date, type of event and guest count on WhatsApp for an offer.

How far in advance should we book?

At least four to eight weeks, so there is time to design the flash sheet. Summer weddings and events should be booked early.

Where in Cyprus do you work?

Limassol and all of Cyprus, including Paphos, Larnaca, Nicosia, Ayia Napa and Protaras. Travel is included in the quote.

Does every guest have to get a tattoo?

No. The station is an option and a highlight. Usually 30 to 60 percent of guests take part.
