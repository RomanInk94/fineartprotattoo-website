My roots in glass craftsmanship

At Derix in Taunusstein, I helped realise the “Simchat Tora” window for the synagogue in Bad Kreuznach, working from a design by René Blättermann. Project report: Jüdische Allgemeine

The windows of Our Lady of the Angels in Arizona are another part of that story. As a member of the studio team, I carried out much of the glass etching. Artist Scott Parsons describes our collaboration in his account of the project. Project report: Scott Parsons

Today: personal art on skin

Today, I bring that attention to detail to people and their tattoo ideas. Fine-line lettering, miniature tattoos and spiritually inspired conceptual designs connect precise visual work with personal meaning. Working on skin is a responsibility I take seriously. Every piece begins with listening to your story. As a traveling artist, I welcome enquiries from Germany and around the world.
