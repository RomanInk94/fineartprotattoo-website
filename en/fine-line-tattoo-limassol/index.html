Placement, size and ageing

Every tattoo softens over the years, and very fine lines are no exception. That is why size and spacing matter: lines placed too close together can merge with time. Roman plans each fine line design with enough breathing room so it still looks clean years from now.

Stable skin is ideal for fine line work: forearm, upper arm, shoulder blade, collarbone, spine or ankle. Fingers and palms are not recommended, because fine lines fade quickly there.

Fine line in the Cyprus sun

Sunlight is the biggest enemy of fine lines. With long summers and strong UV in Cyprus, protect a healed tattoo with SPF 50 whenever it is exposed.

During healing, about two weeks, keep the tattoo out of the sun, the sea and the pool entirely. If you are visiting Limassol on holiday, book your session towards the end of your stay.

Fine line tattoo questions

How long does a fine line tattoo take?

Small fine line pieces often take one to two hours, including stencil placement. Larger botanical or lettering pieces take longer. You receive a time estimate with your quote.

Do fine line tattoos hurt?

Most clients describe fine line work as very bearable because the needles are thin. Sensitivity depends on placement: ribs, inner arm and spine feel more intense than the forearm or upper arm.

Do fine line tattoos last?

All tattoos soften over time. Fine line tattoos last well when they are designed with enough size and spacing, healed properly and protected from the sun with SPF 50.
