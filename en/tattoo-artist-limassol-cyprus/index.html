What is a microrealism tattoo?

Microrealism is a tattoo style that renders portraits, statues, animals or nature photographically realistic at a small scale. It relies on very fine needles, soft black-and-grey shading and precise proportions, so it suits detailed motifs of roughly 5 to 15 cm.

What is a concept tattoo?

A concept tattoo is a custom design built around a personal story or idea rather than a ready-made motif. Symbols, geometry, lettering and realistic elements are combined into one composition that carries your meaning. Roman develops each concept design with you before the session.

Do you tattoo Greek statues and Stoic motifs?

Yes. Fine art designs inspired by classical sculpture and Stoic philosophy, such as Greek gods, Marcus Aurelius, Prometheus, Icarus or Michelangelo’s David, are a focus. They can be realised in microrealism or as part of a larger concept piece.
