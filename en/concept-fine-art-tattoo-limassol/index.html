From your idea to a tattoo

Bring a memory, an object, a phrase or an image you keep coming back to. Roman works with you to choose a central motif and decide how the supporting elements sit around it. A concept can use a single symbol or bring several ideas into one composition.

Placement affects the shape of that composition. A forearm design can follow the length of the arm. A piece for the upper arm can use the curve of the shoulder. Fine lines give the elements room around them, while black-and-grey realism can give a face, animal or object depth.

Roman’s work in stained glass and art glazing informs his use of light and line. If a classical bust or myth is central to your idea, explore his statue and philosophical tattoos.

Questions about a custom design

Can a concept tattoo combine fine line and realism?

Yes. Fine lines can frame the composition or connect its elements, and black-and-grey shading can give a central motif depth. Roman will advise on the size needed for the detail you want.

What should I send for a quote?

Send a short description of the idea, the placement, an approximate size and any reference images you have. The quote depends on the size, detail and placement of the design.

Where is the studio and which languages do you speak?

The private studio is in Limassol, Cyprus. You get the address with your appointment confirmation. Consultations are available in English, German and Russian.
