Walk-in & same-day tattoos in Limassol

Want a tattoo today or this week? FineArt Pro Tattoo works from a private studio in Limassol, so walk-ins run through WhatsApp: send your idea, and if a slot is free you can come in the same day. Short-notice sessions work best for small fine line tattoos, lettering and minimal designs.

How walk-ins work

  1. Message on WhatsAppSend your idea, a reference, the size, the placement and when you are free. A photo of the area helps.
  2. Get a time and a priceIf a slot is free today or in the next days, you get a time, the price and the studio address.
  3. Come inBring a valid ID, eat beforehand and wear clothes that give easy access to the placement.
  4. AftercareYou leave with aftercare instructions. Keep the tattoo out of the sun, sea and pool for about two weeks.

Walk-in from Paphos, Larnaca or Nicosia

Looking for a walk-in tattoo in Paphos? Limassol is about 50 minutes away by car via the A6. From Larnaca it is about 45 to 50 minutes, from Nicosia about an hour.

Message before you set off so the slot is reserved for you. If you are on holiday in Cyprus, plan your tattoo for the last days of your trip, so you do not lose your beach days.

Walk-in questions

Do you take walk-ins in Limassol?

Yes, via WhatsApp. Because the studio is private, message first: if a slot is free, you can come in the same day.

Can I get a tattoo today?

Often, yes, for small fine line tattoos and lettering. Send your idea, size and placement on WhatsApp and you will hear whether a slot is free today.

What can I get as a walk-in tattoo?

Small fine line designs, minimal symbols, lettering and matching tattoos of roughly 3 to 8 cm. Microrealism and concept pieces need an appointment.

How much is a small walk-in tattoo?

You get the price on WhatsApp before you come, based on size, detail and placement. No surprises at the studio.

How old do you have to be to get a tattoo in Cyprus?

At FineArt Pro Tattoo you must be at least 18 and bring a valid ID or passport to your appointment.

Do you speak Russian or German?

Yes. Roman speaks Russian fluently, and consultations are also possible in English and German.
