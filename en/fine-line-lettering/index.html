Your words, your handwriting, your expression

You can begin with a name, a short phrase, initials or a piece of personal handwriting. You may already have a clear idea, or you may know the feeling before you know the design. Together, we explore a direction: restrained and simple, flowing and expressive, or accompanied by a small symbol.

Spiritually inspired ideas can become part of the design when they connect with your own story. What matters is the meaning the piece holds for you.

Turning an idea into an individual design

To start your enquiry, send the exact wording, your preferred placement, an approximate size and one or two references. We can then discuss letter shapes, proportions and orientation. Each of these choices helps the finished design feel considered and personal.

Questions about your lettering design

Can you work from personal handwriting?

Yes. Send a clear reference so we can discuss the features that make it distinctive.

Can I add a symbol?

Yes. A small symbol can support the meaning when it works with the lettering.

Do I need to choose a typeface first?

No. References help communicate your preferences; we can develop the direction together.

How do we choose the orientation?

We consider the placement, reading direction and length of the text together.
