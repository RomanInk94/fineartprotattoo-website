Fine line, miniature tattoos & concept work

My work focuses on fine-line lettering, miniature tattoos, microrealism and personal conceptual designs. Spiritually inspired ideas and the stories behind them have a particular place in my practice. I bring a background in glass craftsmanship and a thoughtful interest in the people involved in each project.
